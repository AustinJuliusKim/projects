# Canon roster — 120 foods

Design pass, 2026-08-06. The 25 already scoped (3 written, 22 drafted from
research packs) plus 95 designed here. This is the authoring queue, not
finished content — every food still needs its own sourced record.

Built from nutrition-category reasoning and culinary knowledge, with hazard
classifications checked against FDA/EPA, AAP and CDC. **No competitor food set
was consulted**, which is the point: their selection and arrangement is
protected, and copying it is exactly what we're avoiding.

**Why this shape.** Animal protein at 24 is deliberately inverted from the
typical infant food set, where meat is three or four entries buried under
twenty fruits — iron and zinc are the limiting nutrients at six months.
Vegetables lead on count because that's where texture and bitterness variety
lives, and eight of them also carry non-heme iron. Fruit is capped at 15:
enough for within-category generalization, not enough to make this a fruit
list. Herbs and spices are a real category at 11 — there is no evidence base
for withholding them and they're the cheapest flavour variety available.

**Iron: 58 of 120.** 24 heme (every animal protein), 14 legumes/seeds, 8
grains, 8 leafy or sea vegetables, 4 nut/seed butters.

**Cultural: ~55 of 120 non-Anglo-American.** Korean is the largest thread
(~40), then Japanese/Chinese/SE Asian (~12), Indian (~8), Latin American (~8),
Levantine (~8), plus Ethiopian, West African and Mediterranean singles. They
are distributed across all nine categories on purpose.

> **Do not sort or group the app's food list by cultural tag.** The whole point
> of the distribution is that these aren't an "international" section.

## Flag list 1 — needs careful writing, not a line item

**Mercury — an exclusion sub-list.** FDA/EPA "Choices to Avoid": shark,
swordfish, king mackerel, Gulf tilefish, bigeye tuna, marlin, orange roughy.

> **The king-mackerel trap.** *King* mackerel is on the avoid list; *Atlantic
> or chub* mackerel (고등어) is a Best Choice. The canon must never have a
> single entry called "mackerel". Separately, "ahi" on a menu is frequently
> bigeye. Albacore/"white" and yellowfin tuna are Good Choices at one serving
> per week — not avoid — which is why the roster uses canned *light* skipjack.

**Choking, intrinsic rather than incidental**, within this roster: 연근 lotus
root (crisp through any cooking), 콩나물 and 숙주 (long strings), shiitake caps
(rubbery, slippery), edamame (skin pops off as an intact disc), grapes and
blueberries (round and firm), 대추 and prune (pit plus indigestible skin), corn
kernels, whole olives, and any nut or seed butter served unthinned. **The
dilution rule applies to four entries and deserves its own explainer.**

**Unpasteurized and raw.** Raw milk and raw-milk cheeses (queso fresco, feta,
some labneh), unpasteurized juice or cider, raw honey, raw sprouts, raw or
undercooked shellfish — 굴 especially, since raw oysters are a normal adult
food — raw fish (회, sashimi), raw or undercooked egg (homemade mayo, aioli,
batter), undercooked meat and poultry, and home-canned or home-fermented
low-acid foods.

**High-sodium preserved.** 김치, 된장, 고추장, 간장, miso, fish sauce and 액젓,
젓갈, salted/oiled commercial 김, 멸치, 단무지, olives, salted canned beans and
broths, bouillon, furikake, commercial za'atar, hard salted cheeses, and cured
meats — bacon, ham, prosciutto, sausage, and **Spam 스팸**, worth naming
explicitly for a Korean-American household rather than hiding under "cured
meats". Practical target: well under ~400 mg sodium/day; pull the baby's
portion of any braise before seasoning.

**Hard age restrictions.** Honey before 12m (incl. 유자청, granola, honey butter
chips). Cow/goat/plant milk *as a drink* before 12m — yogurt and cheese *as
food* are fine from 6m, and this distinction confuses people constantly. Juice
before 12m. Added sugar before 24m (rules out sweetened 팥, 유자청, sweetened
yogurt). Whole nuts, popcorn, hard raw vegetables, whole round fruits until ~4
unless modified. Rice milk (arsenic). Caffeine — though 보리차 barley tea is
caffeine-free and a normal Korean toddler drink.

**Toxicology and physiology inside this roster.** Cassava (cyanogenic
glycosides). 고사리 (ptaquiloside — the traditional prep *is* the mitigation).
토란 (oxalate raphides). Liver (preformed vitamin A is cumulative — the only
entry needing a frequency cap). 감 (unripe astringent varieties, bezoar risk at
volume). Potato (solanine when green or sprouted). 김 vs 미역 both carry iodine,
but 다시마 kombu is an order of magnitude worse and should be named as the real
outlier. Beets/spinach/chard nitrates, plus harmless beeturia. **Star anise** —
Japanese star anise is neurotoxic and has repeatedly adulterated Chinese star
anise; not on the roster but families meet it in braises.

**Allergen classification traps.**
- **Molluscs (굴, 홍합, 바지락, 가리비) are highly allergenic but are NOT among
  the nine US major allergens** — crustacean is. Families reasonably assume
  "shellfish" is one category. Needs its own paragraph.
- **Coconut** — FDA classifies it as a tree nut for labeling; clinical
  cross-reactivity is rare. Same confusion, opposite direction.
- **메밀 buckwheat** — a labeled major allergen in Japan and Korea, not the US.
  A family with Korean relatives needs the asymmetry.
- Avocado, banana, kiwi — latex-fruit cross-reactivity / oral allergy syndrome.
- Vanilla extract is alcohol-based.

**The highest-leverage nutrition note in the product:** calcium suppresses
non-heme iron absorption and vitamin C raises it. Don't schedule yogurt
alongside the lentil meal; do pair the bell pepper. This is encodable.

**Mechanical:** pin bones in 고등어, cod, whole fish; shell fragments in crab
and shrimp.

## Flag list 2 — deliberate exclusions

| Excluded | Why |
|---|---|
| Shark, swordfish, king mackerel, Gulf tilefish, bigeye tuna, marlin, orange roughy | FDA/EPA "Choices to Avoid" — methylmercury |
| Honey and honey-containing products before 12m | Infant botulism |
| Whole nuts and seeds; popcorn | Choking — AAP withhold until ~4 |
| Hard raw vegetables; raw apple chunks | Choking |
| Whole grapes, cherry tomatoes, blueberries; hot dog and sausage rounds | Choking — round-and-firm geometry |
| Marshmallows, hard/sticky candy, gum; cheese chunks and string cheese | Choking (AAP-named) |
| Unpasteurized milk, cheese, juice, cider; raw sprouts | Listeria, E. coli, Salmonella |
| Raw or undercooked shellfish, fish, eggs, meat, poultry | Foodborne illness |
| Cow/goat/plant milk as the primary drink before 12m | Renal solute load, iron displacement, GI blood loss |
| Fruit juice before 12m | AAP |
| Added sugar before 24m | Dietary Guidelines / CDC |
| Added salt, soy sauce, fish sauce, bouillon as seasoning | Sodium |
| 김치 before ~12m | Sodium + chili + 젓갈; rinsed 백김치 is the later on-ramp |
| Cured meats incl. Spam 스팸 | Sodium + nitrites |
| Rice milk | Inorganic arsenic |
| Star anise tea | *Illicium anisatum* adulteration |
