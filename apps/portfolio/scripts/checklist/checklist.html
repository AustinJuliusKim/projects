Small Business Tech Checklist

Tick what is true today. Anything unticked is worth a look. About 10 minutes.

Website & Google profile

  • My website loads in under 3 seconds on my phone.
  • The site works well on a phone: text is readable, buttons are easy to tap.
  • My hours, address and phone number match everywhere (site, Google, Yelp).
  • My Google Business Profile is claimed and I can log in to it.
  • Customers can order or book on my own site, not only through a third-party app.
  • I know how much I paid in delivery-app or booking-platform fees last month.
  • I own my domain name and know who to call if the site goes down.

Network & Wi-Fi

  • Wi-Fi reaches every room where staff or customers use it.
  • Customers use a guest network, separate from my staff network.
  • My POS, cameras and printers are not on the guest network.
  • The router admin password is not the one printed on the sticker.
  • My router and Wi-Fi access points get firmware updates.
  • I have a simple list of my devices, my internet provider and its support number.

Security & backups

  • Multi-factor authentication is on my email, bank and website accounts.
  • Each person has their own login; nobody shares one password.
  • I use a password manager instead of reusing passwords.
  • Important files are backed up in a second place automatically.
  • I have tested restoring a file from backup in the last year.
  • Staff know not to click unexpected links or pay unexpected invoices without a call.
  • Computers and phones install updates, and former staff no longer have access.

Accessibility (ADA / Unruh risk)

  • Images on my site have short descriptions for screen-reader users.
  • I can use the whole site with just a keyboard (Tab and Enter).
  • Text is easy to read against its background colour.
  • Forms have visible labels and clear error messages.
  • My site does not rely on an accessibility overlay widget as its fix.
  • Someone has checked the site against WCAG 2.1 AA.

California's Unruh Civil Rights Act lets customers claim damages over inaccessible sites. Overlay widgets do not remove that risk; fixing the site itself does.
