# anthropic-academy — 2026-11-01

## Summary

Anthropic Academy released three new foundational courses with concrete mental models for AI collaboration:

- **AI Fluency course** (14 lessons) introduces the **4D Framework** (Delegation, Description, Discernment, Diligence) as a structured approach to prompting and task delegation
- **AI Capabilities & Limitations course** (13 lessons) builds accurate mental models of what LLMs can/cannot do: knowledge cutoffs, context limits, steerability, working memory
- **Human-Agent Teams course** (5 lessons, beta) positions Claude as a multiplayer AI for team workflows
- **Product ecosystem expansion**: Claude Code, Cowork, Tag, and Platform are now framed as integrated surfaces for delegation and human-agent collaboration

**Why it matters**: These courses codify Anthropic's official teaching positions on effective AI use. The 4D Framework and capabilities/limitations model are directly teachable in guided-repl's 5-minute format and address common mental-model gaps that today's prompted lessons may not cover explicitly.
